# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Super Tab Sorter Next · Updated / 更新日期：2026-10-22

## 中文

本扩展在浏览器内读取打开的标签页网址、标题、ID、固定状态及标签组信息，仅用于排序、分组排列和重复标签页去重。扩展不保存浏览历史，也不向开发者或其他服务发送标签页数据。

偏好设置（排序设置、你填写的正则去重规则、自动排序、休眠扩展 ID、语言及安装版本标记）通过 `chrome.storage.sync` 保存；如果启用了 Chrome 同步，Chrome 可能通过你的 Google 账号在设备间同步这些设置，由 Google 的隐私政策管理。扩展从打开的标签页读取的网址和标题不写入同步存储；你自行填写在规则中的内容作为偏好设置同步。

扩展没有分析统计、广告、追踪器或远程执行代码。致谢中的外部链接仅在你主动点击时打开，目标网站适用其自身的隐私政策。

你可以关闭自动排序或修改设置。卸载会移除本地扩展数据；Chrome 账号同步数据的管理遵循 Chrome 的同步设置。

对通过 Google API 获取的信息的使用遵守 Chrome Web Store 用户数据政策，包括“有限使用”要求。

隐私问题可在[本项目的 GitHub Issues](https://github.com/tongsun99/super-tab-sorter-next/issues) 中提出。请勿在公开 issue 中附上私密网址或个人数据。[项目源码](https://github.com/tongsun99/super-tab-sorter-next)。

## English

The extension reads open-tab URLs, titles, IDs, pinned status, and tab-group information inside your browser solely to sort, arrange groups, and remove duplicate tabs. It does not store browsing history or send tab data to the developer or other services.

Preferences (sorting settings, user-entered regex deduplication rules, auto-sort, suspender extension ID, language, and installation/version flags) are stored using `chrome.storage.sync`. If Chrome sync is enabled, Chrome may synchronize these settings across devices through your Google account, subject to Google's privacy policy. URLs and titles read from open tabs are not written to sync storage; content you enter into rules is synchronized as part of your preferences.

There are no analytics, ads, trackers, or remotely executed code. External attribution links open only when you click them; the destination's own privacy policy applies.

You may disable auto-sort or change settings. Uninstalling removes local extension data; account-synced data is managed through Chrome sync settings.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For privacy questions, open an issue at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/tongsun99/super-tab-sorter-next/issues) without including private URLs or personal data. [Project source](https://github.com/tongsun99/super-tab-sorter-next).
